Amendment 03 (lodged 2026-06-08) redefines the editorial-convention component of the H2.1 temporal deconvolution-mixture and reframes what the convention artefact is. A full template-dictionary scan of the corpus showed the lodged reign-and-round-period convention basis to be empirically inadequate (multi-century calendar slabs are about 31 percent of the convention pool and were absent from it, while reigns are only about 2.7 percent), so the convention component is rebuilt as an empirical, frequency-weighted three-tier calendar-slab basis (sub-century, century, multi-century), with no reign tier and built per frame; reigns, dynasties, and datable events are reclassified as genuine-but-aoristic information rather than convention; and the convention artefact itself is reframed as the grid-quantisation of genuine-but-coarse evidence onto the BC/AD calendar lattice (the per-item distortion and cross-item boundary pile-ups the method removes) rather than as an absence of chronological information, with decadal and quarter-century brackets carried as a reported sensitivity band. The change binds the H2.1 temporal mixture only and is independent of Amendments 01 and 02, which govern the cross-sectional track on date-window counts; the mixture likelihood, the learned three-tier structure, the analysis envelope, the observation model, and year-precise dates are unchanged. Because the validated recovery of the earlier basis does not automatically transfer to a multi-century-bearing basis, the change was gated on a fresh recovery re-validation (a stress-triage followed by a full 450-cell synthetic-recovery grid, scored under the Amendment-01 criterion), which passed: 96.4 percent of in-envelope cells recover cleanly against the 90 percent bar, the feared confusion of a multi-century plateau with genuine quiescence is absent, and the convention-fraction recovery precision stays within the shape-conditioned envelope Amendment 01 established. The full amendment is reproduced in this registration's amendment justification and hosted, version-pinned, at the project repository (git tag osf-amendment-03-2026-06-08); the original supplementary remains the lodged authority for all unchanged specifications.
