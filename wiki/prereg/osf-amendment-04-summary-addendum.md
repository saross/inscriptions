Amendment 04 (lodged 2026-06-14) replaces the editorial-convention deconvolution of the H2.1 temporal mixture with a cross-classified time x alignment model, reversing Amendment 03's single shared convention basis while retaining the rest of Amendment 03 (the grid-quantisation reframe and the reclassification of reigns, dynasties, and datable events as genuine-but-aoristic). After the production fit under the shared basis, a diagnostic showed that one fixed empire- or Latin-wide convention template under-identifies the convention fraction for temporally-concentrated frontier provinces, whose conventional and genuine dating cluster in the same window (their Roman occupation period, roughly AD 100 to 300): the shared template cannot tell the two apart, so the convention fraction collapses for those units (Moesia inferior returned about 0.05 when roughly 60 percent of its dating is conventionally grid-aligned). The amendment brings in a second, independent signal that the timing ignores — whether each inscription's date sits on the round-number calendar grid — and models each unit's grid-aligned and non-aligned inscriptions jointly, so the contrast between the convention-enriched aligned subset and the genuine-enriched non-aligned subset identifies the convention fraction that the timing alone cannot. The convention shape is a fixed, data-independent library of round-endpoint calendar slabs, identical for every unit, and the alignment rates are re-derived from the corrected estimates; the diagnosis and remedy are standard statistical and archaeological practice (weak-separation identifiability, Feller et al. 2016 and Gustafson 2010; the classification covariate as a likelihood term, Huang and Bandeen-Roche 2004, with the OxCal outlier model as the archaeological archetype, Bronk Ramsey 2009). Because it changes the model's likelihood structure, the change was recovery-validated on synthetic data with known answers — a 300-cell grid that eliminates the shared basis's systematic over-attribution and pulls the confounded cells to truth — before a production refit of the 29 analytical units, which recovers sensible convention fractions for the previously-collapsed frontier provinces while leaving the well-identified units (such as Pompeii, correctly near zero) unchanged; a prior-sensitivity sweep confirms the per-unit estimates are robust to the alignment-rate assumption for 27 of the 29 units. The mixture likelihood's temporal structure, the analysis envelope (50 BC to AD 350), the observation model, year-precise dates, and the cross-sectional track (Amendments 01 and 02) are unchanged. The full amendment is reproduced in this registration's amendment justification and hosted, version-pinned, at the project repository (git tag osf-amendment-04-2026-06-14); the original supplementary remains the lodged authority for all unchanged specifications.
