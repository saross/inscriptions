# EJA companion — women's crossover-age marriage history (stub)

**One-line.** The *substantive* demographic history that the JAMT paper's women
case study deliberately does **not** attempt: a time-resolved account of the
Shaw–Saller crossover age (wife-vs-daughter commemoration) across the empire,
targeted at the *European Journal of Archaeology* (EJA).

**Why this is a separate paper.** In the JAMT methods paper, the
wives-and-daughters corpus is the **thematic-subset exemplar** for de-fogging as
a *diagnostic* (Act II) — and the honest verdict there is that the corpus is
~90 % editorial convention and **below the reachability floor**, so it cannot
carry time-resolved demographic claims on its own (this is the "Option 2"
case study). The crossover-age *history* — the substantive payoff — needs
better-dated data and Adela's domain lead, and so is reserved for this EJA
companion ("Option 3").

**This is a stub, not a plan yet.** It exists so the three future-paper threads
(this, the HMM follow-up, the Aeneas-partition paper) have a common home under
`wiki/planning/future-papers/`. Substantive scoping begins after the JAMT
paper is drafted.
