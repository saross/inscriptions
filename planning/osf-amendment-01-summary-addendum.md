Amendment 01 (lodged 2026-06-XX) extends this protocol with a two-measure
framework: epigraphic acts (inscription count, the unchanged primary-by-convention
measure) and epigraphic content (letter mass), promoting letter mass from an
exploratory cross-check to a co-registered parallel confirmatory measure for H3a,
with each unit forming its own confirmatory family and no cross-unit multiplicity
correction. It additionally (i) clarifies the H2.1 recovery-grid binding criterion
— reporting the grid as a recoverability map with a stated operating envelope
(alpha ≤ 0.70), patching the undefined flat-shape Pearson case with Wasserstein-1,
and demoting exact alpha-coverage to a shape-conditioned diagnostic — and records
the realised two-grid outcome (the inscription-mass grid passes and the
letter-mass grid fails its convergence precondition, so the temporal mixture
deconvolution is validated for inscription mass only); and (ii) pre-specifies that
subset production timelines (summed probability analyses, SPAs) are de-fogged by
a subset-specific mixture fit (the model learns each subset's own
cataloguing-convention mix) rather than by
imposing the empire-wide convention shape, gated by a measured reachability floor
(worst-case N ≈ 2,000 within the operating envelope). The full amendment is the
accompanying file; the original supplementary remains the lodged authority for all
unchanged specifications.
