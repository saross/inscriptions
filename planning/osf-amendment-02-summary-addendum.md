Amendment 02 (lodged 2026-06-06) makes the Latin-speaking provinces of the corpus the primary frame for the cross-sectional hypothesis-testing analyses (the H3a within-province population effect, the H3b deviation-detection, the H3c Hanson-replication tests, and the SR1 scaling comparator), and reports the empire-wide analysis as secondary context. The rationale is dataset coverage: LIRE is a corpus of Latin inscriptions, approximately complete for the Latin-speaking western provinces but capturing only a non-representative minority of epigraphic production in the Greek-speaking east, so an empire-wide frame mixes well-covered and poorly-covered provinces, which is a coverage artefact rather than a fact about the ancient world. The change is coverage-driven, not result-driven: the preregistration's own "~815 cities" sampling figure was already the Latin-province filter, the empire-wide results are retained and reported rather than withdrawn, and no model, estimand, decision rule, or threshold changes. The amendment additionally reconciles the lodged document's 41-Latin-province figure with the realised 39-province analysis frame (two provinces classify Latin but contribute no population-matched cities, and one is a spelling normalisation to the dataset's own field values) and shows the reconciliation changes no result. The full amendment is reproduced in this registration's amendment justification and hosted, version-pinned, at the project repository (git tag osf-amendment-02-2026-06-06); the original supplementary remains the lodged authority for all unchanged specifications.
