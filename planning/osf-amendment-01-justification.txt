This is the first amendment to the project's preregistration (osf.io/uycs6, lodged 2026-05-20, embargoed). It is filed under the preregistration's own contingency rule, which requires substantive post-lodgement methodology changes to be lodged as an OSF amendment before implementation. It is a binding gate: Stage 3 confirmatory fits under the revised framework do not run until this amendment is lodged.

The amendment makes three connected changes.

(1) Two-measure framework. The lodged protocol treated letter count as a single exploratory cross-check on inscription count. This amendment promotes letter mass (epigraphic content — how much was inscribed) to a co-registered parallel confirmatory measure for H3a, alongside inscription count (epigraphic acts — how often a community inscribed), which remains the primary-by-convention measure and is unchanged. Because the two units operationalise different constructs, each forms its own confirmatory family with no cross-unit multiplicity correction, and their per-act difference (the content residual) is added as a pre-specified descriptive derived quantity. The promotion is construct-driven, not result-driven: the confirmatory decision rules for letter mass are identical to the pre-existing inscription-count rules, and no rule is tuned to any observed letter-mass result.

(2) Recovery-grid binding-criterion clarification. Adjudicating the completed recovery simulation exposed two mathematical/asymptotic defects in the lodged validation criterion as written: Pearson r is undefined for the flat genuine shape (correlation against a constant target), and exact credible-interval coverage of the mixing weight collapses at large N for reasons unrelated to recovery. The criterion is corrected — the flat case is patched with Wasserstein-1, Pearson r >= 0.95 is retained for all non-flat shapes, the mixing weight is reported as a shape-conditioned diagnostic rather than a gate, and the grid is reported as a recoverability map with a stated operating envelope (alpha <= 0.70). Under the corrected criterion the inscription-mass grid passes and the letter-mass grid fails its convergence precondition, so the temporal mixture deconvolution is validated for inscription mass only. This is anticipated by change (1): the letter-mass confirmatory family was already bounded to the cross-sectional H3a, which does not use the temporal deconvolution, so the letter-mass grid's failure reinforces the existing scope rather than altering it.

(3) Subset deconvolution mechanism. The amendment pre-specifies that subset production timelines are de-fogged by a subset-specific mixture fit — the model learns each subset's own cataloguing-convention mix from the subset's own data — rather than by imposing the empire-wide convention shape on every subset. Subset deconvolutions are gated by a measured reachability floor (worst-case N of about 2,000 inscriptions within the operating envelope) in addition to the existing detection-power thresholds.
